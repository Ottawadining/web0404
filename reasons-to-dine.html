10 Reasons to Dine
in Ottawa

Canada's capital doesn't just have politics and museums — it has one of the country's most underrated food scenes. Here's why it belongs on your itinerary.

1. World-class tasting menus

Ottawa's fine dining scene punches well above its weight for a city this size — modernist tasting counters, French-trained kitchens, and rooms that rival what you'd find in Toronto or Montreal, often at a fraction of the wait for a table.

2. Patios with a view you can't get anywhere else

Where else can you sip a drink beside a UNESCO World Heritage canal, watch boats glide past your table, or look out over Parliament Hill from a rooftop? Ottawa's best patios turn a meal into a postcard.

3. A genuinely international food scene

From Little Italy's trattorias to some of the country's best sushi and omakase counters, Ottawa's diversity shows up on every menu in the city.

4. Brunch worth setting an alarm for

Whether it's a line-out-the-door scratch diner or brunch beneath the turrets of a century-old castle hotel, Ottawa's weekend brunch scene is reason enough to plan a trip around a Saturday morning.

5. Steakhouses with real history

Some of Ottawa's best steakhouses have been serving the city since the 1960s and 70s — heritage buildings, live-fire cooking, and old-school hospitality that hasn't changed with the trends.

6. A pizza renaissance in full swing

Since 2016, a wave of Neapolitan- and Detroit-style spots has transformed Ottawa into a genuine pizza destination — the kind of scene that gets locals arguing about their favourite slice.

7. Great food doesn't have to break the bank

A 24-hour diner institution, a Chinatown pho spot locals swear by, $5 banh mi — Ottawa's cheap eats scene proves you don't need a big budget to eat exceptionally well.

8. The ByWard Market

One of Canada's oldest public markets sits at the centre of the city — fresh produce by day, some of Ottawa's best restaurants and rooftop bars by night, all within a few blocks of each other.

9. Walkable food neighbourhoods

Little Italy, Preston Street, Elgin Street, Wellington West — Ottawa's best eating is concentrated into a handful of walkable strips, so a single evening can turn into a proper food crawl without ever needing a car.

10. It's the capital — so there's always something else to do

Parliament Hill, world-class museums, the canal skate in winter, festivals all summer — Ottawa's food scene is reason enough to visit, but it's backed up by everything else a capital city has to offer.
